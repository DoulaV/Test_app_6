# Merge sheet: V1 (Power of Suggestion) + V2 (Idea Mining)

Both are Kallaway. V2 is the research process (find validated ideas); V1 is the
quality bar each idea must pass to build an obsessed personal-brand audience.
Together: mine, then shape.

## Agreements (the spine)

- Relevance to one specific viewer is the first gate. V1 element 1 "narrow until it feels personal" (V1 t=19:13); V2's first filter "is this interesting or relevant to me" and niche-specific patterns (V2 t=19:33, t=07:17).
- Ideas must be grounded in evidence, not gut. V1: "get results first, then create content", proof over opinion (V1 t=21:17); V2: ten reps on validated data beat ten on gut (V2 t=05:36).
- Study what already works and beat its weak points. V1's worked examples deconstruct his own outliers; V2 makes it the method (V2 t=03:52, t=20:16).
- Non-obvious wins. V1 element 3 (V1 t=20:10); V2's angle remix aims to make a better version than the outlier (V2 t=06:45).

## Single-source points (kept, attributed)

- V2 only: seven legos; outlier score and thresholds (5x, 0.5%); 2x2 sourcing rule; watchlist seeding methods; daily vs long-term logging; hold/remix; the presenter's default hold set.
- V1 only: the bank shot (never claim superiority, show results); three levels of obsession; contrast frame, relatable character, small cost large reward; quantitative proof and atomic steps; the five-element checklist with problems and fixes.

## Tension (do not average away)

V1 says "high signal does not come from saying the same thing over and over
that other people say with just different editing" and "if you're recycling
the same ideas from other people in your space... you're never going to build
an obsessive audience" (V1 t=05:59, t=06:15). V2 says find outliers in your
niche and remix them, holding two legos constant (V2 t=06:08).

Resolution: they operate on different legos. V2's hold set is structural
(hook style, visual format, sometimes topic); V1's non-obvious requirement is
about the idea itself, which lives in the angle. So: borrow structure from
outliers (V2), and make the angle non-obvious by colliding the topic with a
framework or fact from outside the niche (V1). A remix that keeps the
outlier's angle is the recycling V1 warns against; a collision with no
validated structure is the gut-guessing V2 warns against. The combined skill
requires both, and says which lego each rule governs.

## Second, smaller tension

V2's presenter default keeps the topic and changes the angle, hook, and
visual format (V2 t=20:43), while his general rule keeps hook style and
format (V2 t=06:24). He says it changes by niche. The skill states the general
rule (hold whatever the pattern across winners shows is constant) and lists his
default as one example, not the rule.

## Structure

- Opens with a single named concept plus credentials; defines it with a paired good/bad example inside 90 seconds.
- Holds attention with a ladder (three levels, each harder), one worked example of his own per level, and a recurring refrain ("I never once said...").
- Turns at 03:36 (framework), 08:33, 13:33 (each level), 18:25 (collapse into five elements).
- Closes with the five-element checklist and the claim that all three levels run at once when all five are present.

## Procedure (as taught)

1. Narrow the topic until it feels made for one specific viewer. (V1 t=05:27, t=19:04)
2. Build every piece around a non-obvious idea produced by colliding something from outside the niche with a framework you already own. (V1 t=05:47, t=06:34, t=20:34)
3. Validate every claim with proof the viewer can verify; get the result first, then make the content. (V1 t=06:48, t=21:17)
4. To shift beliefs, frame with contrast (before/after), deliver through a relatable character, and make the path look like small effort for a large reward. (V1 t=10:00 to 11:45)
5. To drive action, show quantitative proof (specific numbers mapped to a result) and steps broken to the atomic unit; never command. (V1 t=14:05, t=15:12, t=15:56)
6. Never state your own superiority; state results and method and let the viewer conclude it. (V1 t=01:07 to 01:54, t=07:42, t=12:36, t=17:43)

## Structure

- Opens with the thesis (ideas decide performance) and immediately redefines the unit of work (seven legos).
- Holds attention with concrete arithmetic (outlier score), a thought experiment (beat 100 videos), a 2x2 with a pause-and-guess, then a screen-share with exact settings.
- Turns at 08:33 (theory to tactics) and 12:30 (manual to tool).
- Closes with a recap, a hand-off to a future "idea development" video, and offers.

## Procedure (as taught)

1. Think of every idea as seven legos: topic, angle, hook (spoken, text, visual), story structure, visual format, key visuals, audio. (V2 t=00:48 to 02:55)
2. Build a watchlist of 20 to 30 top accounts in the niche (and adjacent niches separately), seeded from an existing list, from who a great account follows, from platform search, or from a tool database. (V2 t=12:50 to 15:06)
3. Pull outliers: same content type (short vertical video) on Reels, TikTok, Shorts; outlier score 5x and above; engagement 0.5% and above; date window by content type; sorted by outlier descending. Check most days. (V2 t=08:33 to 11:18, t=16:17 to 17:10)
4. Analyze each outlier across the seven legos, watching it and reading its transcript, logging daily inspiration per topic and long-term patterns per structure. Apply the "is this interesting and relevant to me" filter first. (V2 t=17:19 to 20:25)
5. Find the pattern across winners, hold the winning legos constant, remix the rest, and target the outlier's weak points to beat it. (V2 t=04:15 to 06:50, t=20:33)
6. Then develop: reassemble from hook, story structure, and visual format databases. (V2 t=22:15)

## Inferences and gaps

- (gap) No frames or captions; Gemini-only.
- (gap) The tool (Sandcastles) does the aggregation; the skill must give a manual equivalent (a spreadsheet of accounts, each account's median views, per-video ratio) since users may not have the tool.
