## Procedure (as taught)

1. Decide the topic and the target viewer; write line 1 and 2 to state the topic plainly and make them lean in via one of four levers (common ground, benefit or pain point, metaphor, mind-blowing fact). (t=01:17 to 02:45)
2. Write one contrast line beginning with "but" (or however, yet, although, on the other hand) that stops the reader without paying off. (t=02:46 to 03:55)
3. Write the snapback: a claim that reverses the direction of the lean while staying on topic; bigger reversal, stronger hook. Make sure the payoff Z is real. (t=03:56 to 04:57, 08:05)
4. Distill the context lean into three to five bold on-screen words; choose an opening visual with calibrated motion. (t=09:31 to 11:35)
5. Reframe the lean around a desire or pain the viewer already has, and anchor unfamiliar topics to known brands, people, or cultural moments. (t=11:36 to 13:49)
6. Deliver a first hit of value inside or right after the hook; front-load. (t=13:50 to 15:03)
7. Rewrite the hook sentences short and staccato. (t=15:04 to 15:45)
