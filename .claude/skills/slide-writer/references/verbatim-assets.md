## framework (presentation styles and document types) (V17 t=02:00, V21 t=06:30, V21 t=10:15, V21 t=11:45, V21 t=12:45)

```
Keynote (Steve Jobs style): pictures, a few words; needs a speaker.
Team alignment / informal: brief and simple; no storyline polish.
Jour fixe (recurring cross-team): standing agenda; quality matters because it is forwarded.
Board / senior leadership: nail it; basic language, larger type, no nitty-gritty.
External (investors, banks): board standard plus legal and PR review.
Documentation: detailed, lots of text, fully self-standing.
```

## definition (V01 t=03:00)

```
An action title is a header of a slide that summarises the content of the slide by formulating a complete sentence.
```

## example (action title ladder) (V01 t=09:00, V01 t=10:00, V01 t=11:00)

```
Derive potentials for an expansion  (topic title, not an action title)
Expanding to new geographies creates a positive EBIT potential  (generic)
Expansion to North America and Asia Pacific creates a positive EBIT potential  (specific)
Expansion to North America and Asia Pacific creates a positive EBIT potential of 22 EUR million  (quantified)
Expansion to North America and Asia Pacific with 22 EUR million EBIT potential  (concise: the target)
```

## example (situation, complication, solution) (V13 t=11:00)

```
The profit margins of our company have been steadily declining over the last five years. In addition, new competitors have entered our home market this year, putting additional pressure on our market share. What can we do to address this issue? This report outlines how a shift from product selling to solution selling could defend our market share and increase our profitability. We will highlight what processes and capabilities we need to make this happen and how the implementation roadmap looks.
```

## cheat sheet (chart selection) (V04 t=06:30, V04 t=07:30)

```
Component comparison (shares of a total): pie (five parts or fewer) or stacked column
Absolute comparison: bar or waterfall
Time series: line or column
Distribution and correlation: histogram, scatter, bubble (three dimensions)
```

## checklist (self-explanatory chart) (V23 t=04:00, V23 t=08:30, V14 t=09:00)

```
Chart title: what am I looking at
Unit of measurement for every dimension
Percent of what
Date or period of the data
Legend for any colour or size coding
Source, internal ones included
Any other qualifier (survey geography, FX-adjusted or not)
```

## checklist (what AI-built slides get wrong) (V10 t=05:15, V10 t=06:30, V10 t=07:30, V10 t=13:15)

```
Accent bar or box running into the footer
Text or bars not aligned with the action title's left edge
No space between bullet and text
Wrong dash for ranges
A template element copied without the content that justified it
Inverted colour hierarchy (darker should mean higher level)
Parallel items described with different sub-headings
Title not in the template's title style, font size drifting
```

## term (V07 t=01:00, V07 t=02:00)

```
Mickey Mouse master (also: strawman): a paper sketch of the whole deck, one box per page with a draft action title and a rough chart, made by the lead and handed to the team, or by the junior to align with the lead before building.
```
