## definition (t=01:10)

```
Dopamine is not the pleasure chemical, it's the prediction chemical. Dopamine fires in your brain when you're trying to predict what might happen next. Not when you're getting a reward, but when you're anticipating getting it.
```

## definition (re-hook) (t=05:37)

```
The re-hook is the moment where one loop closes and another loop opens at the same time. There should never be a gap.
```

## quality rule (head fake) (t=11:05)

```
A cheap surprise is just random. It comes out of nowhere, it doesn't have anything to do with the lead up, and it just doesn't make any sense. A great head fake is one that you would never have expected, but as soon as you hear it, you immediately understand why it's true.
```

## checklist (stakes) (t=14:35, t=16:00)

```
Three things to make the stakes matter for anyone in any story:
1. a character someone can root for
2. something at risk
3. urgency
Before you start your story, the viewer has to know: what is at risk, who it's happening to, why it matters right now.
```

## framework (head fake) (t=18:40)

```
Step 1: figure out what the viewer will naturally predict based on the context.
Step 2: deliver an answer or reveal that contrasts what they believe, but is clearly logical in a way they can accept to be true right away.
```

## worked example (looped version, moves labelled) (t=08:30, t=09:32, t=10:20, t=11:41)

```
[STAKES] Once upon a time, there was a prince, and his kingdom was dying. A plague had been spreading for months across the country, and it had almost reached the town where his castle and his family were staying. The only cure for this plague is a flower that grows at the top of an ancient tower, buried deep in the mountains. But the tower is guarded by a dragon that hasn't been defeated in thousands of years. Every knight that has gone to try to defeat this dragon and get this flower has never come back. But the prince has to go. His father, the king, is begging him to. If he doesn't, everyone he loves will be dead by the end of the week.
[BIG QUESTION] The prince arrives at the base of the tower, and the dragon is massive, ten times the size of what he thought. He has one sword, no armor, and no backup. How is he possibly going to get past this thing?
[HEAD FAKE, clue: dragon pulling away, scared] The prince draws his sword and starts approaching the dragon. But as he gets closer, he notices something. The dragon isn't attacking him. It's pulling away. It's scared. And then he sees it: a massive chain around the dragon's neck, bolted to the base of the tower. The dragon isn't guarding the tower, it's a prisoner of the tower. He puts his sword down, walks up to the chain, and breaks it. The dragon stretches its wings for the first time in centuries and flies away. The path to the tower is wide open.
[RE-HOOK] He climbs to the top floor and the magical flower is sitting in the middle of the room. But standing next to it is the king, his father, the one who sent him on this mission in the first place.
```
